### The same reader, three pages (KRouter 1.3.0)

Moved here from the README when KRouter went to five pages; these rows measure the three-page version and were not
rerun for five ([set 11](../bench/memory_honesty/HELDOUT.md#set-11-longmemeval-answer-accuracy-scored-the-way-other-projects-report-it-registered-2026-11-09-before-any-run), [set 12](../bench/memory_honesty/HELDOUT.md#set-12-the-same-reader-given-different-things-registered-2026-11-09-before-any-run)).
LongMemEval S, 500 questions; reader and judge Claude Sonnet 5.5 with the benchmark's own prompts.

| The reader is given | Right, of 500 | Of the 470 answerable |
|---|---|---|
| nothing | 30 (6.0%) | 0 |
| plain keyword search (BM25), its top three sessions | 405 (81.0%, 77.4–84.4) | 376 |
| the pages KRouter 1.3.0 cites, three at most | 400 (80.0%, 76.4–83.4) | 371 |
| the benchmark's own answer sessions: perfect retrieval | 492 (98.4%, 97.2–99.4) | 462 |

At three pages KRouter's lookup was level with plain keyword search (−1.0 points, paired interval −2.8 to +0.8), and
the 18 points missing against perfect retrieval were lost in retrieval, mostly on multi-session (69 → 117 of 121) and
temporal (97 → 125 of 127) questions, where three pages miss a session. Every reply and verdict:
[`bench/longmemeval/results/`](../bench/longmemeval/results/).
