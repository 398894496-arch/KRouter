# Lesson: When you are stuck and cannot see the problem, write it out for someone else

- Situation: Spent an afternoon on a bug with no idea what was wrong
- Action: Started writing an issue for a colleague, step by step: how to reproduce, what was expected
- Result: At step three, noticed the arguments were passed in the wrong order
- Lesson: When you are stuck and cannot spot what is wrong, write the whole story step by step as an explanation for an outsider; the problem often shows itself while you write
