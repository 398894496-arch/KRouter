# Lesson: When there are piles of errors, fix the first one

- Situation: A build printed more than 300 lines of errors
- Action: Looked only at the first one: a missing header
- Result: With that added, all the other errors disappeared
- Lesson: When errors come in a flood, most of the later ones follow from the first; fix only the topmost one first
- When: a wall of errors; hundreds of errors; error after error
