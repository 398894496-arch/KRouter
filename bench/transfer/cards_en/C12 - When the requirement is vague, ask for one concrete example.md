# Lesson: When the requirement is vague, ask for one concrete example

- Situation: A client wanted the poster to look more premium; three revisions were all wrong
- Action: Asked them for one reference image they considered premium
- Result: Only the image showed they meant lots of white space and few words
- Lesson: When someone cannot say what they want and only that it feels wrong, stop revising on guesses and ask them for one concrete example
- When: cannot say what they want; vague request; it just feels off
