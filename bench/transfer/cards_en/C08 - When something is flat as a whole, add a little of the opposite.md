# Lesson: When something is flat as a whole, add a little of the opposite

- Situation: The braised pork was salty enough but tasted flat
- Action: Added a small spoon of sugar at the end
- Result: The flavour got depth at once, and the saltiness came through more clearly
- Lesson: When something is flat as a whole and lacks depth, do not add more of the same; add a little of something that contrasts with the main note
- When: flat; bland; missing something; lacks depth
