# Lesson: When something drags on without progress, set a time limit and ship a simple version

- Situation: Spent two days trying to shave another 10 ms off an endpoint
- Action: Gave it half a day; at the deadline shipped the version that was already good enough
- Result: The simple version went out and no user noticed the 10 ms
- Lesson: When a task has taken a long time without progress, give it a time limit and ship a simple version that is good enough when the time is up
